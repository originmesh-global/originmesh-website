THE CHALLENGE

Supply chains move.
But does the evidence?

Products pass through many hands, places, and systems before reaching a buyer. Yet the information describing their journey can remain fragmented across spreadsheets, documents, field records, and disconnected platforms.

When information is incomplete or difficult to connect, it becomes harder for supply-chain participants to understand, review, and communicate a product's journey.

01 / FRAGMENTED DATA
⌘

Disconnected records

Important information can live in separate tools, files, and workflows.

02 / DATA QUALITY
◎

Incomplete information

Missing fields, inconsistent records, and errors can make evidence harder to use.

03 / LIMITED VISIBILITY
⌁

Hard-to-follow journeys

Connecting origin details to later supply-chain stages can be challenging.

OUR APPROACH

Connect the data.
Strengthen the evidence.

ORIGINMESH is being explored as an evidence and data-quality layer that can work alongside existing systems—not as a replacement for official platforms or certification authorities.

ABOUT ORIGINMESH

An early-stage idea
with a global ambition.

ORIGINMESH is an independent technology initiative exploring how better-connected data and evidence can support more transparent supply chains.

It is currently in its research and discovery stage. We are speaking with exporters, cooperatives, supply-chain professionals, and other stakeholders to understand where the greatest needs exist before defining and building a solution.
